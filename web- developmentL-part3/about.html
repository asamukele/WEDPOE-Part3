WHO WE ARE

About The Matric Dance Foundation

Building confidence, creating opportunities and making matric memories possible.

OUR STORY

Everyone deserves their special moment.

The Matric Dance Foundation is a non-profit organisation created to support learners who come from financially disadvantaged backgrounds.

For many learners, the cost of a matric dance outfit, hair styling and other preparations can make attending the event difficult.

Our goal is to bring schools, volunteers, businesses and donors together to provide practical support where it is needed most.

Our Vision

A future where every learner can celebrate their achievements regardless of their financial circumstances.

Our Mission

To provide resources, services and opportunities that allow underprivileged learners to participate in their matric dance with dignity and confidence.

OUR VALUES
