The Matric Dance Foundation is a non-profit organisation helping underprivileged learners access the clothing, beauty services and support they need to attend their matric dance with confidence.

OUR MISSION

More than a dance. It's a memory.

Financial circumstances should never prevent a learner from celebrating one of the most important milestones of their school journey.

Dance Clothing

We help learners access suitable matric dance outfits without placing financial pressure on their families so they can attend their matric dance with smile on their face.

Beauty Services

We connect learners with donated hair, beauty and styling services for their special evening.

Community Support

Volunteers, businesses and donors work together to give learners an unforgettable experience.
